Micro Finance Scheme (MFS)

Direct micro-credit loan assistance for Scheduled Caste entrepreneurs to start or expand income-generating small activities.

Interest Rate 6.5% p.a.
Max Amount ₹1,25,000
Repayment Period 3 Years
Moratorium 3 Months

Who can apply (Eligibility)

  • Applicant must belong to the Scheduled Caste (SC) community.
  • Annual family income must be below ₹3,00,000 per annum.
  • Applicant age should be between 18 and 65 years.
  • Project must be an eligible small business, service, or tailoring activity.

Documents to bring

  • Valid Caste Certificate issued by an authorized state authority.
  • Income Certificate / Self-declaration of family income.
  • Aadhaar Card or Government Issued Identification.
  • Bank Account details (Passbook copy or cancelled cheque).

How to apply

  1. Check your eligibility using the instant button below.
  2. Gather the required documents listed above.
  3. Apply online through the PM-SURAJ portal or visit your nearest functioning State Channelizing Agency office.

Wondering if this loan fits you?

We check your profile against NSFDC's official rules and show you exact repayment costs or specific reasons if you don't qualify.
